# `what-so-what-now-what` — fact, meaning, next step

Use this framework to interpret data, results, or LLM output without confusing observations with inferences.

1. **What / what we know:** present only available facts and, when relevant, their source.
2. **So what / what it means:** explain justified implications. Identify uncertainties; do not turn correlation into causation or interpretation into fact.
3. **Now what / what we do:** propose actions only when requested or when sufficient data supports them. Distinguish a recommendation from an obligation. If there is no basis, state that the source does not define the next step; do not invent one.

Check: Does each implication follow from what was observed? Is uncertainty preserved? Is the next step proportionate and consistent with warnings and conditions?
